# Building Agents

This section covers building and running Agents in Heicode: the quickstart and creating your first Agent, understanding task views and status, configuring permissions, approval and secret safety, connecting resources and documents, choosing a working mode, and monitoring, delivering and iterating after launch. Multi-Agent collaboration in Swarm mode has its own subsection, and common problems are collected in the FAQ.
